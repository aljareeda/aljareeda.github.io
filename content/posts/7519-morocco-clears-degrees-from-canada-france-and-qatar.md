## 🎓 Three foreign degrees accepted for equivalence: Qatar, France, and Canada/USA

If you studied abroad, whether your degree counts in Morocco decides which jobs and professions are open to you. In this issue the Higher Education Minister, Azzedine El Midaoui, signed three decisions on 20 May 2026 that accept specific foreign credentials for equivalence with Moroccan degrees. Each followed a review by the national equivalence committee, which met on 23 April 2026.

- **Qatar → Moroccan bachelor's (*licence*) in law.** A bachelor's degree in Law and Police Sciences from the Police Academy (College of Police) in Qatar.
- **France → Moroccan "State Engineer" diploma.** The *Titre d'ingénieur diplômé* of the Conservatoire national des arts et métiers (CNAM), civil engineering, specialty buildings and public works.
- **Canada + USA → Moroccan "State Engineer" diploma.** A master's in civil and environmental engineering from the University of Western Ontario, paired with a bachelor of science in civil engineering from Wentworth Institute of Technology (USA). The Canadian master's and the US bachelor's are listed together as one qualification.

These decisions name individual credentials rather than opening a general door. The text does not describe the steps for each person who holds one of these degrees. Anyone with a similar degree should check with the Ministry of Higher Education.

*Sources: Higher Education Minister's decisions nos. 991.26, 992.26 and 993.26, dated 3 Dhul Hijjah 1447 (20 May 2026).*

## 💧 Land seizures advance for the Bouaouane dam in Chichaoua province

The government has declared the Bouaouane dam a "public utility" project and ordered the expropriation of private land for it in the commune of Sidi Ghanem, Chichaoua province. This issue carries the second part of the decree. It runs about 70 pages of schedules listing each plot, its owner, and what grows on it, which is mostly olive and carob trees. A local public inquiry ran from 22 January to 22 March 2025 at the commune's offices. For families with land or relatives in the area, the tables are the concrete part of the story.

The pages I reviewed do not state the dam's capacity, its cost, or the compensation owners will receive, so none of that is reported here. The decree was signed by Prime Minister Aziz Akhannouch and Equipment and Water Minister Nizar Baraka on 4 June 2026.

*Source: Decree no. 2.26.389 of 18 Dhul Hijjah 1447 (4 June 2026).*

## 🗳️ The Left Alliance gets its ballot symbol for the 23 September 2026 elections

Moroccans will vote in legislative elections for the House of Representatives on 23 September 2026. The Interior Minister, Abdelouafi Laftit, has assigned a ballot symbol to the "Left Alliance" (Tahalouf al-Yassar), a joint list from the Democratic Left Federation and the Unified Socialist Party. Their symbol is "the Letter and the Candle." In Morocco's election system, a symbol on the ballot helps voters recognize a list. For these two parties it formalizes their joint run.

*Source: Interior Minister's decision no. 1190.26 of 2 Muharram 1448 (18 June 2026).*

## ⚖️ A senior judicial official is named to head the tax-violations committee

The Prime Minister has amended the membership of the committee that reviews tax violations. Named as chair is Mounir El Montasser Billah, an advisor at the Court of Cassation (Morocco's highest court) and Secretary General of the Superior Council of the Judiciary. The change was made on the Council's proposal. The published text is abridged and does not show the full member list or the term of appointment, so the rest of the committee's makeup is unclear. A committee that reviews tax disputes affects how taxpayers can challenge fines and penalties.

*Source: Head of Government's decision no. 3.28.26 of 15 Dhul Hijjah 1447 (1 June 2026).*

## 🏥 Eight health-training institutes get a new framework, effective from the 2024–2025 school year

The Health Minister, Amine Tahraoui, replaced the three annexes governing Morocco's public health vocational-training institutes. The new annexes cover locations and seat capacity, the list of courses, and the training programs. The eight institutes are in Casablanca, Rabat, Fez, Marrakech, Oujda, Agadir, Tangier and Laayoune. For most of them the table shows 300 seats in one track and 240 in the other, 540 in total. The Casablanca and Rabat rows are hard to read in the source, so their figures are not reported here.

Courses cover emergency transport and ambulance work, surgical-unit assistant, medical equipment sterilization, ER reception, and care for infants, children and the elderly. The ambulance course totals 2,500 hours over two years, including internships. The decision applies from the 2024–2025 academic year, which means it is being published after that year has started.

*Source: Health Minister's decision no. 347.26 of 20 Sha'ban 1447 (9 February 2026).*

## 🏘️ Two local development plans move forward: Saada (Marrakech) and Ouled Ali Mansour (Tetouan)

A decree approves a new development plan for the commune of Saada, near Marrakech, and declares it a matter of public utility. It replaces the 2021 plan for the commune's center. The approval followed a public inquiry from 22 October to 20 November 2025, and the commune's council met on 27 November 2025. In Tetouan province, the regional governor approved a rural development plan for the center of Ouled Ali Mansour, after a public inquiry from 15 September to 16 October 2025. The first is plan PAC.SAADA.2024 and the second is PDAR04/2025 AUTE. These plans decide what can be built and where, which matters to anyone with family land in either area.

*Sources: Decree no. 2.26.347 of 25 Dhul Hijjah 1447 (11 June 2026); Wali's decision no. 852.26 of 29 Shawwal 1447 (17 April 2026).*

## 🚆 A railway level crossing in Rhamna is removed

The Transport Minister, Abdessamad Kayouh, removed the second-class level crossing no. 6051, where a public road crossed the Sidi El Aidi–Marrakech rail line at kilometer point 145+790, in Skhour Rehamna commune, Rhamna province. The decision does not say why. Crossings are usually removed when a road is rerouted or a bridge or underpass replaces them, but that is not stated here.

*Source: Transport Minister's decision no. 964.26 of 24 Dhul Qi'dah 1447 (12 May 2026).*

## 🏫 Land for a new middle school in Tangier

The Economy and Finance Minister, Nadia Fettah, authorized the transfer of a plot of land in Tangier for a new middle school, Ibn Tawit Al-Tanji. The plot has two enforced mortgages in favor of the Tangier City tax office, ranked first and second. The decision follows a public inquiry from 21 May to 23 July 2025. The plot's size is not legible in the pages reviewed.

*Source: Economy and Finance Minister's decision no. 971.26 of 25 Dhul Qi'dah 1447 (13 May 2026).*
