# Morocco's New Cabinet Is Official: Bourita Keeps the Diaspora File

*Sourced from the Kingdom of Morocco's Official Gazette (Bulletin Officiel), issue №7554, dated 22 October 2026 (10 Jumada al-Awwal 1448). This aljareeda translates and prioritizes the parts that actually matter to Moroccans abroad and outside observers — not the routine bureaucratic paperwork.*

## 🏛️ The new government is named — and one minister already works on your file

Morocco now has its official cabinet. A royal decree names the full team that will run the country's ministries under Head of Government Fatima Zahra Mansouri, who was herself appointed by a separate royal decree on 1 October 2026. The appointments take effect from **16 October 2026**, five days before the decree was signed on 21 October. The decree says the ministers were proposed by the head of government and took their oath before the King.

**For Moroccans abroad**, the key line is that Nasser Bourita is named Minister of Foreign Affairs, African Cooperation and Moroccans Residing Abroad. The decree gives him responsibility for the diaspora brief in the title of his portfolio. It does not say what policies will follow.

The decree names **19 full ministers**, **11 delegate ministers** (junior ministers attached to a senior minister) and a Secretary General of the Government, Hassan Tarek. One of the 11 delegate ministers was relieved within days (see below). Portfolios that matter most to readers with family, land or business in Morocco:

## 🛡️ National defense administration placed under the head of government

A second royal decree hands Mansouri the day-to-day running of Morocco's National Defense Administration. She exercises it under the King's authority, in his role as Supreme Commander of the Royal Armed Forces, and the arrangement runs from 16 October 2026. The decree says she must follow the rules already in force.

She may in turn let the delegate minister in charge of the Defense Administration, Abdellatif Loudiyi, and the administration's staff sign or countersign most decisions. **Decrees and regulatory orders are excluded** from that delegation. The decree cites the organic law governing the government's work and a 2023 decree on delegating signature authority.

This is also a power-structure story: it spells out who can sign what within a defense body. The text does not say what the administration does day to day, so we leave that out.

*Source: Royal Decree (Dahir) No. 1.26.92 of 21 October 2026, published in the Official Gazette, issue №7554.*

## 🔄 A minister is relieved within days of the cabinet taking effect

Madiha Khayer, named delegate minister for Vocational Training and Qualification under the National Education minister, is relieved of her duties by a third royal decree. The relief takes effect on **18 October 2026**, two days after the cabinet's start date of 16 October. The decree says it acts at the head of government's request. It gives **no reason**.

Her departure leaves 10 of the 11 delegate ministerial posts in the cabinet list filled. The decree does not say whether anyone will replace her.

*Source: Royal Decree (Dahir) No. 1.26.93 of 21 October 2026, published in the Official Gazette, issue №7554.*
